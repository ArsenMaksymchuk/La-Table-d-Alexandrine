# La Table d'Alexandrine — design brief

- **Design read:** A refined Marseille bistro presentation for guests discovering a French family restaurant, warm, tactile and confident rather than formal or ostentatious.
- **Concept spine:** *La table comme un rituel*. Every section feels like another moment around one carefully set table: welcome, first taste, the menu, the room, shared impressions, then the practical details of a visit.
- **Delivery tier:** spectacle. The scroll-scrub film is the primary cinematic mechanic; bespoke CSS 3D perspective, layered parallax and a tactile menu-card interaction create a second dimensional beat. No generated 3D mesh is claimed or required.
- **Palette:** cream `#F5F0E7`, warm beige `#E7DCCB`, dark walnut `#30251F`, muted gold `#A48758` (the single accent), porcelain white `#FFFEFA`. This explicitly follows the user's named cream, beige, brown, gold and white palette; the otherwise-banned beige-and-brass family is therefore an intentional user override. Gold is reserved for small highlights, rules and interactive states.
- **Typography:** Metropolis ExtraBold for expressive display, system Arial/Helvetica for readable body copy, Courier New for tiny utility labels. The display voice is editorial but contemporary; no default luxury-serif cliché.
Animation mode: animated-website
- **Journey shape:** single-shot. One continuous ~15-second camera move is the right story for a restaurant: a composed French bistro table resolves into a close, tactile view of one plated dish. Multiple worlds would add seams without adding meaning.
- **Journey:**
  1. **Bienvenue:** wide establishing view of a warm walnut table, linen and porcelain; headline “Bienvenue à La Table d’Alexandrine”; one short welcome line.
  2. **Le plaisir du détail:** the camera glides closer to a plated French bistro dish; copy introduces convivial French cooking without inventing provenance or ingredients.
  3. **La carte:** the dish resolves into a close-up; the menu section presents illustrative dish examples, not a verified current menu, with all prices and availability marked for confirmation.
  4. **À table:** the visual rests on tableware and atmosphere; a gallery uses generated demonstration imagery, never presented as photographs of the actual restaurant.
  5. **Les avis:** the journey settles; no fabricated testimonials or ratings, only a clear place for verified Google and Tripadvisor integrations.
  6. **Nous trouver:** final quiet frame and transition to contact, map, preliminary hours and social link, all marked for owner confirmation.
- **World grammar:** French bistro still-life, one walnut tabletop, cream linen, ivory porcelain, muted gold cutlery, warm natural window light from upper-left, deep walnut shadows, gentle film grain, restrained highlights, consistent lens and color grade. No people, readable text, logos or watermarks in generated footage.
- **Mobile framing:** every food focal point remains centered in the central 60% of frame; a portrait 404×720 mobile encode; all chapter copy remains in semantic document flow and the film has a static poster fallback.
- **Delivery budget:** desktop video ≤32 MiB; mobile video ≤16 MiB. One encoded clip per device profile.
- **Section plan:** (1) hero / image-first cinematic stage; (2) restaurant introduction / editorial offset; (3) menu / asymmetric typographic menu spread; (4) gallery / diagonal staggered masonry; (5) reviews / quiet split editorial panel; (6) visit / contact dossier with map and hours. No repeated layout family; the footer is integrated into the visit section.
- **Asset plan:** one six-frame continuous-move storyboard; one 15-second scroll-scrub demonstration film assembled from its keyframes with gentle zooms and transitions (not a generated live-action video); exact desktop/mobile encodes and first-frame posters; six cropped demonstration stills; four generated section reference boards (hero, maison, menu, reviews); one HTML typographic wordmark only (no invented official logo); one Pillow-composed OG card; favicon, Apple touch icon, 192/512 and maskable icon kit. No custom icon sheet or additional atmospheric plates were produced because the workspace generation allowance was exhausted. All generated food and table images carry a visible “VISUEL DE DÉMONSTRATION” label in the site; one interior photo slot remains explicitly marked “Photo à ajouter”.
- **CTA inventory:** nav “La carte” (moving hairline); hero “Découvrir la carte” (walnut block with arrow) and “Nous contacter” (outlined link); menu category selectors (segmented editorial tabs); contact phone links and “Construire un itinéraire” (underlined route link); footer “Facebook” (plain external link). Each action has its own treatment; no shared global button class.
- **Trust and accuracy:** demo-only notice visible on the page; opening status not asserted; example menu names identified as illustrative and not a verified current menu; prices and availability show “Prix à confirmer”; address and both phone numbers show “À confirmer par l’établissement”; hours are explicitly preliminary; no fake reviews or unverified rating; generated imagery is not described as actual restaurant photography.
- **SEO:** title “La Table d’Alexandrine | Cuisine française à Marseille 13008”; description describes a demonstration presentation and explicitly notes that menu, contact details and hours require confirmation. Natural copy targets restaurant français Marseille, restaurant Marseille 13008 and cuisine française Marseille without asserting an unverified terrace or neighborhood.
- **Previous-build differentiation:** no earlier shipped website exists in this build session. Identity axes are locked here: warm paper-and-walnut palette, Metropolis/Arial, image-first cinematic hero, A4 scroll-scrub, stamp/route/ticket CTA garments, soft 14px corner language.
